Material and setup

Measure the kerf

Coupon cut fileCoupon locator

Import at 1:1 in millimetres with controller kerf compensation off. Each square cut yields one plug and its matching opening. Keep track of the face, site and original orientation. On the same face, measure the inside of the opening and the outside of the plug parallel to each page axis, away from the corners. Use the same calipers throughout.

SiteAxisOpening (mm)Plug (mm)Kerf (mm)

Kerf = (opening − plug) / 2. Check the two axes and all three sites for variation before interpreting fit.

Six cut families

Sampler cut fileStyle locator

The 297 × 160 mm sheet holds six 90 × 70 mm coupons with ten pieces each. Keep each coupon’s parts together. Count pieces damaged during release. Assemble the coupon flat, then separate and reassemble it. Record any chipped projection, split neck, jam or unexpectedly loose joint and its location. Use the same handling sequence for each style. The family names link to individual cut files for recuts.

Shop observations

Matched board trial

A cut fileA seam locatorB cut fileB seam locator

Import each 368 × 368 mm board at 1:1. Match stock lot, grain, face, settings, artwork and support. With plain wood, leave both faces plain. Mark each board’s top edge on its tray. Record sheet positions and cut order. Keep the design key and locators with the observer during a timed solve.

Whole board

Count pieces damaged at release. Time each solve from the first piece handled to the last piece placed. Record elapsed time for an unfinished attempt and mark it unfinished. Use the same sorting rules and time limit. Alternate A-first and B-first across solvers and record each solver separately.

For a whole-board movement observation, use the same tray, orientation, frame condition and handling action on both boards. Describe the action, then record the number or location of pieces that moved. The seam measurements below concern individual pairs.

Marked seams

Use each locator to select the two adjacent pieces at S1 through S4. Isolate each pair on a level surface. Record how it mates on the first assembly. Aim for ten hand assembly and separation cycles per pair. Stop if it breaks and record the completed cycles and the affected piece. At the first and last cycle, try sliding one flat piece away while holding the other in place. Record whether it separates in-plane or needs a lift. Record the direction tried and use the same handling on both boards.
