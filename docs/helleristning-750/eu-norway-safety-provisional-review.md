## 2. Critical Jurisdiction Split: EU vs. Norway (EEA)

Although Norway participates in the European single market via the EEA Agreement, **EU regulations do not apply automatically in Norway** until formally incorporated into the EEA Agreement by an EEA Joint Committee Decision and transposed into Norwegian national law. Therefore, **domestic Norwegian sales** and **sales/exports into EU Member States** currently face **distinct legal baselines** and enforcement authorities.

## 3. Detailed Technical & Legal Analysis by Topic

### 3.1 Toy Safety vs. Adult Puzzle Classification (750 Pieces, 14+)
1. **Legal Exemption Threshold:** Under **Directive 2009/48/EC Annex I, item 8** (and Norway's **Leketøyforskriften**), *"puzzles with more than 500 pieces"* are expressly excluded from the definition of a toy. With **750 pieces**, this product is structurally classified as an adult/collector puzzle.
2. **Conditions to Preserve the Exemption (Anti-Toy-Drift Controls):**
   - Do **not** market, illustrate, or categorize the product as a children's toy, kids' gift, or educational children's game.
   - Label clearly on the box exterior and online product listings: **"14+ — Adult Collectible Wooden Jigsaw Puzzle / Puslespill for voksne og ungdom (14+)"**.
   - Include a clear small-parts choking hazard notice (not an EN 71 toy-directive logo if avoiding toy misrepresentation, or confirm exact wording with compliance counsel):
     - **EN:** *"CAUTION: Contains small wooden pieces. Choking hazard — not suitable for children under 3 years. Intended for ages 14+."*
     - **NO:** *"ADVARSEL: Inneholder små deler. Kvelningsfare — ikke egnet for barn under 3 år. Beregnet for 14+ år."*
   - **NO CE Mark:** Because a 750-piece non-toy wooden puzzle is not covered by any CE-marking EU harmonization legislation (not a toy, not electrical, not PPE, not machinery), affixing a `CE` mark is **legally prohibited** under Regulation (EC) No 765/2008 Art. 30(2).
3. **Voluntary Mechanical & Chemical Margin (Best Practice):** Even though EN 71 is not legally mandatory for a 750-piece 14+ puzzle, applying **EN 71-1** (splinter-free edges, mechanical neck/tip integrity) and **EN 71-3** (heavy-metal migration limits for any wood stains, lacquers, or inks) as internal quality benchmarks provides strong evidence of "general safety" under GPSR Art. 5 & *Produktkontrolloven* § 3.

### 3.2 EU GPSR (Regulation (EU) 2023/988) Checklist (Mandatory for Any EU Sales)
For any unit placed on or shipped to the EU market (including e-commerce shipments from Norway to EU consumers):
- [ ] **Internal Risk Assessment & Technical Documentation (Art. 9(2)):** Retained for **10 years** after the last unit is placed on the market. Must document: mechanical splinter/sharp-tip risk on laser-cut birch wood, smoke/char residue wipe-down, choking hazard from small pieces (~20–40 mm spans), adhesive/formaldehyde off-gassing, and box/bag suffocation risks.
- [ ] **Traceability Marking on Product/Packaging (Art. 9(5)–(6)):**
  - Type, batch, or serial number (e.g., `HELL-750-ED01-B2026-01`).
  - Manufacturer legal name, registered trade name, postal address, and **electronic contact address** (email or contact URL).
- [ ] **EU Responsible Person (Art. 16 GPSR):** Because Norway is an EFTA/EEA state (and until GPSR is formally incorporated into the EEA Agreement), a Norwegian manufacturer selling directly into the EU **must confirm with legal counsel** whether an **EU-established Responsible Person** (importer, authorized representative, or fulfillment provider within the 27 EU Member States) must be named on the packaging and online listings.
- [ ] **Distance / Online Offer Disclosures (Art. 19 GPSR):** Every webshop listing serving EU buyers must display: manufacturer details, EU Responsible Person details, clear product image & identifier, and all safety warnings/age grading in the local language.
- [ ] **Incident Reporting & Recall Readiness (Art. 20, Art. 35–37):** Documented procedure for logging customer safety complaints and notifying authorities via the **Safety Business Gateway** if an accident occurs.

### 3.3 Birch Plywood Adhesives, Laser Char, Finishes & REACH Compliance
1. **Formaldehyde (REACH Annex XVII Entry 77 — Reg. (EU) 2023/1464):**
   - Limit for wood-based articles effective **6 August 2026**: **≤ 0.062 mg/m³** measured per REACH Appendix 14 reference chamber conditions (approx. half the historical E1 standard of 0.124 mg/m³).
   - **Action Required:** Require supplier documentation confirming **REACH Entry 77 compliance (≤ 0.062 mg/m³)** — e.g., **NAF (No Added Formaldehyde)**, **ULEF**, **EN 636 interior/exterior phenol-formaldehyde or bio-adhesive birch plywood**, with third-party test reports (e.g., EN 717-1 / ISO 12460-1 / Appendix 14 correlation).
2. **Laser Cutting Residue & VOCs:**
   - Laser cutting birch plywood produces surface char and phenolic/wood-smoke odor along kerf walls.
   - **Production Requirement:** Specify masked cutting (low-tack paper transfer tape) or post-cut dry/light-abrasive tumble cleaning, plus an airing/off-gassing dwell period before sealing inside the box so pieces do not stain fingers or emit excessive VOC odor.
   - Verify any post-cut oil, wax, lacquer, or pigment ink meets **REACH Annex XVII** (no restricted PAHs, phthalates, azo dyes, or heavy metals) and contains **< 0.1% w/w SVHC** (Candidate List check at date of manufacture).
3. **Splinters, Delamination & Tip Breakage:**
   - Physical safety overlaps directly with our `>= 3.0 mm` neck-width digital screen and supplier calibration coupon (`runs/helleristning-750-commercial/calibration_coupon.svg`): brittle outer veneer plies or snapped narrow rock-art extremities can produce sharp wood splinters.
   - Require **B/BB or B/B grade aviation/laser-grade birch plywood** (void-free inner core, laser-compatible glue line without dark char pockets) and physical drop/handling/flex testing on the calibration coupon + 6 worst-neck seam samples before batch cutting.

### 3.4 Timber Traceability, EUDR & Sanctions Compliance
- **Sanctions (Mandatory Immediately — Both EU & Norway):** Obtain written origin declaration + FSC or PEFC Chain-of-Custody invoice claim proving raw birch logs and veneer plies originate strictly outside Russia and Belarus (e.g., Finland, Sweden, Latvia, Estonia, Lithuania, Poland).
- **EUDR (EU) 2023/1115:** Even though finished puzzles (`CN 9503`) sit outside EUDR Annex I, the laser-cutting manufacturer / EU plywood supplier handling raw birch plywood sheets (`CN 4412`) falls squarely inside EUDR once applicable (**30 December 2026** for plywood/EUTR-legacy operators). Collect the plywood supplier's EUDR Due Diligence Statement (DDS) reference number (or confirm Norwegian/EU domestic compliance pathway) as part of supplier onboarding.
