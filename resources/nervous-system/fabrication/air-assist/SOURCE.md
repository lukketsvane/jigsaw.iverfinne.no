# Nervous System Laminar Flow Laser Air Assist

Nervous System designed this nozzle to carry smoke and debris away from the cut when making wooden jigsaw puzzles. The studio reports testing it on a Trotec Speedy 360 laser cutter and designing the internal opening for a 2-inch lens. [Studio article](https://n-e-r-v-o-u-s.com/blog/?p=8722) · [Publisher’s project and machine notes](https://hackaday.io/project/176034-nervous-system-laminar-flow-laser-air-assist)
